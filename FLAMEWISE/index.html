09 / Scientific integrity

How to read the evidence.

Keep direct observations, surrogate experiments, models, and open questions separate.

Measured vs. modeled

Never label a simulation or prediction as a direct experimental measurement. Preserve original units, variable names, and test conditions.

  • Keep missing values missing.
  • Record uncertainty where reported.
  • Attach citations to every imported record.

Gravity-condition caution

Do not label microgravity or normal-gravity results as direct partial-gravity measurements. Identify analogues and model assumptions explicitly.

  • Distinguish partial-gravity targets from measured conditions.
  • Do not combine unlike experiments without justification.
  • Show source limitations.
